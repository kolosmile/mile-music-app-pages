Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

Mile Music Publishing Tools is an internal publishing automation application used to prepare and publish music visualizer content to connected social media accounts.

Information We Process

The application may process account authorization tokens, connected account identifiers, uploaded media files, captions, titles, descriptions, tags, publishing status, and platform response identifiers needed to publish content.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to authenticate connected accounts, upload and publish media, check publishing status, store operational logs, and troubleshoot publishing workflows.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the connected platform APIs, such as TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube, as required to publish content or maintain publishing status.

Data Storage

Operational metadata and publishing logs may be stored in our internal Directus database. Access tokens and credentials are stored in environment configuration or secure operational storage controlled by the operator.

Data Retention

Publishing metadata and logs are retained for operational auditing and troubleshooting. Tokens can be revoked from the relevant platform account or developer console.

User Control

Connected platform access can be revoked at any time through the relevant platform account settings or developer console. To request deletion of operational data, contact us at the email below.

Security

We use access controls and internal operational safeguards to limit access to publishing credentials, uploaded files, and publishing logs.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact milemusicx@gmail.com.
